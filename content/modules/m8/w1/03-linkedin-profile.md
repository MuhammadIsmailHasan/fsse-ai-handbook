## 🧠 The big idea

LinkedIn is a **search engine with a handshake**. First you must be **findable** (keywords recruiters type), then **credible** in 15 seconds (headline, photo, Featured proof), then **approachable** (clear availability and contact).

## ❓ Why it matters

LinkedIn is one of the main channels recruiters use to find people, alongside job portals like JobStreet, Glints and Kalibrr in Indonesia — and it's the **primary** channel for remote roles at foreign and US companies, where nobody will bump into you at an event.

Three things have to happen in order: you must be **findable** (the keywords they type), **credible** within about 15 seconds (headline, photo, Featured proof), and **approachable** (clear availability, easy contact).

If you're targeting remote work outside Indonesia, say so explicitly in the headline and About — recruiters filter on it, and "open to remote (UTC+7, overlap with US hours)" answers their first question before they ask.

## 📖 Step by step

### 1. Headline (≤ 220 characters)

Think of it as a market stall sign: role, stack, and one piece of proof. "Backend Engineer · Python/Flask/AWS · open to remote" beats "Aspiring developer | Passionate about tech".

Formula: **Target role | core stack | proof or focus**

```text
Full-Stack Developer | Python (Flask, FastAPI) · TypeScript (React, Next.js) · PostgreSQL | Building CareerPilot, an AI job-hunt assistant
Backend Developer | Python · Flask · PostgreSQL · Docker | E-commerce API with payments, 100+ tests
```

Avoid "aspiring" if you have deployed projects — describe what you do.

### 2. About (3 short paragraphs)

```text
I build web products end to end: Python APIs, Next.js frontends, PostgreSQL, Docker and CI. After [previous career / study], I completed an intensive software engineering program and kept going with my own projects.

Recently I built CareerPilot, a job-hunt tracker with a Flask API and a Next.js app (secure sessions, 130+ automated tests, deployed with Docker), and RevoShop, an e-commerce backend with Midtrans payment webhooks and role-based access.

I'm looking for a junior backend or full-stack role in Jakarta or remote. I enjoy [clean APIs / testing / making slow pages fast]. Portfolio: github.com/<you> · Email: you@example.com
```

Specific > impressive. Numbers (tests, users, response times) make claims concrete.

### 3. Featured, Projects, Experience

| Section | What to add |
|---|---|
| **Featured** | CareerPilot live link or demo video, GitHub profile, one LinkedIn post about a hard problem you solved |
| **Projects** | CareerPilot, RevoShop — with dates, skills and a link |
| **Experience** | previous jobs with **transferable** results (e.g. "automated weekly reports with Excel macros, saving 4 h/week"); a bootcamp can go under Education or as a program entry |
| **Education / Licenses** | [Bootcamp name]; relevant certificates (AWS later) |
| **Skills** | 10–15 relevant skills, top 3 pinned (Python, TypeScript, PostgreSQL) — endorsements matter less than projects |

### 4. Settings that matter

- **Custom URL**: `linkedin.com/in/muhammad-ismail-dev`.
- **Photo**: clear face, neutral background; **banner**: simple, optional stack/tagline.
- **Open to Work**: choose job titles, locations (Jakarta, Remote) and start date. "Recruiters only" hides the green frame from your network; "All LinkedIn members" shows it — both are acceptable; pick what you're comfortable with.
- **Location** and **work preferences** set correctly (remote/hybrid/on-site).
- Contact info: professional email; don't publish your phone number publicly.

### 5. Networking that works

One specific sentence about their work beats fifty copy-pasted invites. Messages that show you read something they wrote get answered; mass requests get ignored.

Connection request note (≤ 200 characters for many accounts):

```text
Hi Rina — I read your post on migrating to Next.js at Kopi Data. I'm a junior full-stack dev building a similar stack; would love to follow your work. Thanks!
```

Rules: be specific, give before you ask, never send a CV in the first message, follow up once. Engage with posts from engineers at companies you like — thoughtful comments are visible to their network.

### Choosing your setup

Every option here is a trade-off:

| | ✅ | ❌ |
|---|---|---|
| Public Open to Work frame | more inbound messages | some people see it as a negative signal (increasingly rare for juniors) |
| Broad headline ("Software Engineer") | flexible | fewer keyword matches |
| Specific headline | appears in precise searches, credible | may exclude adjacent roles — use About for breadth |
| Posting often | visibility | time; low-quality posts hurt |

## ⚠️ Common mistakes

- Buzzword About sections without projects or numbers.
- Listing 50 skills including ones you've touched once.
- Different job titles and dates on LinkedIn vs CV.
- Messaging strangers "Please refer me" with no context.

## 🏆 Best practices

- [ ] Headline with target role, stack keywords and one proof.
- [ ] About: story → proof with numbers → what you want + links.
- [ ] Featured: live project, demo, GitHub.
- [ ] Projects and experience with results, not duties.
- [ ] Custom URL; consistent name/photo with GitHub and CV.
- [ ] Open to Work configured with realistic titles and locations.
- [ ] Personalised connection notes; no spam, no copy-paste mass messages.
- [ ] Keep it truthful — interviews will test every claim.

## 🗣️ Explain it

Explain "search engine with a handshake" to a friend who says LinkedIn is "just for posting certificates".

## 📐 Design it

Write your headline (two variants: backend-focused and full-stack-focused), your About section (≤ 1,200 characters) and a Featured list of 3 items. Mark every keyword a recruiter might search.

:::quiz
Show a review rubric

- Headline contains a **role title** used in real job posts and **3–5 stack keywords** from your target listings.
- About's first two lines work alone (they show before "see more").
- At least **two concrete numbers** (tests, endpoints, performance, users).
- Clear **availability**: role type, location/remote, start date if relevant.
- Featured items open without login and show your work in < 1 minute.
:::
